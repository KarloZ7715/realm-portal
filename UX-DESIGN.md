## 2c. Settings

The cache-cleanup deferral and the full-width LANGUAGE card follow Figma note 48-6406 (https://www.figma.com/design/6kBPmTavrfpbceMSUvvWl3?node-id=48-6406).

### In scope for 0.1

- The LANGUAGE card (labelled "IDIOMA" in the UI) spans the full width of Settings, in the normal view and when the page is scrolled.

### Deferred, out of scope for 0.1

- Cache cleanup (the "LIMPIEZA" panel: client cache cleanup) is deferred and out of scope for 0.1. Settings omits that panel in the normal view and when the page is scrolled.

### Button surfaces

- On parchment surfaces, buttons use border `#5A3D22` and focus color `#0F5561`.
